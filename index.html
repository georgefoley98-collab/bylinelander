Questions.

Do you only work on Shopify?

Carts and collection pages are built into Shopify themes, including Online Store 2.0 themes. Pre-sell pages sit on your own subdomain and link to your product pages, so they work with any platform.

We already use a cart app. Can you work with it?

Usually we replace it, which also removes a monthly fee. If there is a reason to keep it, we build around it and say so in the quote.

What do you need from us?

Collaborator access to your store, your average order value and items per order from Shopify analytics, and your best-performing ad. Around ten minutes of gathering.

Will our live store change while you work?

No. Everything is built on an unpublished copy of your theme and only published once you have tested it and approved it.

Can you guarantee a result?

No, and be wary of anyone who does before seeing your data. We show you the reasoning and what these builds have done on our own brands, and we tell you if we think a build will not pay for itself.

Who owns the code?

You do. There is no lock-in and nothing to renew.
